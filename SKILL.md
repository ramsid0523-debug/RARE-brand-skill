## What "rare" means

**Rare = maximization, not elitism. A path of living.**

Making more of what you have, from any starting point.

- ✅ Rare = the college student who goes to class AND lifts AND has a social life
- ✅ Rare = the full-time worker who still trains and still builds
- ✅ Rare = the person who refuses to accept "good enough" as the ceiling
- ❌ Rare is NOT "the elite few who were born to win"
- ❌ Rare is NOT for people who already have it all figured out
- ❌ Rare is NOT scarcity for scarcity's sake

The brand is **anti-elitist at the core.** Never write copy or make decisions that suggest products are reserved for people who have already arrived. **The standard is the floor, not the ceiling.**

## The dual-register operating principle

The brand operates on two layers intentionally. **This is the most important thing on this page.**

| Layer | Look | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Surface (the hook)** | Premium, exclusive, hard-to-obtain, elite-coded. Spare, clinical voice. Magazine-rule design. Gold / ivory / black palette. | Filters *for* engaged customers. Filters *out* those who would dilute the brand. |
| **Depth (the reveal)** | Anti-elitist. Accessible. Maximization for anyone, from any starting point. Warm, hopeful, progress-oriented. | Lands once someone engages. Creates loyalty because the contrast is real. |

### Hard rules for every piece of output

1. **Read your copy on both registers before shipping.** Does it read premium on first pass? Does it never contradict the maximization thesis on second pass?
2. **Never name the dual-register mechanism in customer-facing copy.** Brands that explain their own move weaken it. The tension is felt, not declared.
3. **Never punch down.** No copy that implies the customer hasn't earned the product. No condescension to people figuring it out.
4. **Never imply the products are reserved for the already-arrived.** Aspiration is the hook, not the gate.
5. **The customer is the agent of their own life.** "The work is yours; the standard is ours." Never reverse this.

## The customer

Anyone working to add more to their life:

- College student doing class + gym + social life
- Full-time worker finding time to train and build
- Someone seeking guidance on optimizing performance
- Someone looking for an edge — health, focus, athletic ceiling
- Anyone who has decided their current OK is not OK enough

**NOT the customer:** people handed an exceptional life with all resources. The brand visuals signal toward that aesthetic; the actual audience is broader. The "elite" the surface signals to is the *filter*, not the *audience*.

---

## The enemy

**Being OK with being mediocre.** The shrug. Defeatism. "It's good enough." Accepting current circumstance as a permanent station.

The brand rejects:

- Defeatism — "there's nothing I can do"
- The shrug — "life's not my dream but it's good enough"
- Resignation — believing the gap is unbridgeable
- Permanent stations — accepting your current ceiling as your ceiling

The brand does NOT reject:

- Honesty about being in a hard moment
- Slow, small progress (the maximization thesis assumes constraint)
- Starting from constraint (everyone starts somewhere)

## Voice rules (actionable)

### Tone

- Spare. Confident. Clinical when describing product.
- Magazine-style cadence — sentences carry weight; no filler.
- Authoritative without pompous.
- Direct. No hedging ("perhaps," "we believe," "in our opinion") in factual copy.

### Words and phrases to USE

- "Standard," "verified," "held to," "research-grade"
- "For those pursuing rare"
- "The work is yours; the standard is ours"
- "Maximize," "refuse to settle," "from where you are"
- "Rare is not where you start. It's how you decide to live."

### Words and phrases to AVOID

- ❌ **Hype words:** "amazing," "revolutionary," "ultimate," "best-in-class," "game-changing," "unleash"
- ❌ **Transformation language:** "10x your life," "transform your body," "unlock the new you," "next level"
- ❌ **Marketing fluff:** "we believe in," "we are committed to," "our journey," "industry-leading"
- ❌ **Permission/gating language:** "deserve," "earn the right to," "qualify for" — implies customer hasn't earned it yet
- ❌ **Tribal CTAs:** "join the movement," "are you ready," "kings/queens/legends"
- ❌ **Emoji** in product copy, hero text, brand voice, social captions, or component UI

### Capitalization conventions

- **UPPERCASE** for specs, CTAs ("EXPLORE PRODUCTS," "ADD TO CART"), and category labels
- **Title Case** for headlines
- **Sentence case** for body copy

### Required disclaimers (peptide products only)

- *"For laboratory and research use only. Not for human consumption."*
- Visible but not apologetic. Do not soften with marketing language.

## Worked examples

### Product description

❌ **Bad:**
> Experience the ultimate breakthrough in peptide research with our premium Retatrutide compound. Designed for serious researchers who demand the best, this revolutionary peptide unlocks new possibilities for your research goals.

**Problems:** hype words ("ultimate breakthrough," "revolutionary"), "experience" verb, "premium" stated rather than shown, "designed for serious researchers who demand the best" punches down/gates, "unlocks new possibilities" is fluff.

✅ **Good:**
> Retatrutide. Research-grade peptide compound. Lyophilized, sealed, third-party verified. COA available on request. For laboratory and research use only. Not for human consumption.

**Reasons:** factual, declarative. Premium register lives in the *restraint*, not in adjectives. Disclaimer present without apology.

### Customer support reply

❌ **Bad:**
> Hi friend! We're so excited that you reached out! As a valued member of our amazing research community, we're committed to giving you the best support experience possible! Let's get this sorted ASAP! 🙌

**Problems:** "friend," exclamation marks, "valued member," "amazing community," emoji, hype, infantilizing.

✅ **Good:**
> Thanks for reaching out. Your order #XXXX shipped on [date] via [carrier]; tracking is at [link]. If anything doesn't arrive as expected, reply here and we'll resolve it.

**Reasons:** respectful, factual, action-oriented. Treats the customer as a serious person making a serious decision.

### Social media caption (@life.is.rare)

❌ **Bad:**
> 💪🔥 Time to LEVEL UP your gains, kings 👑 The rare life is calling. Are you ready to answer? Drop a 🦁 if you're committed to greatness! #grindset #peptides #performance

**Problems:** emoji, hype, gendered ("kings"), tribal CTA, hashtag spam, "greatness" cliché, "level up" transformation language.

✅ **Good:**
> The standard is the floor. What you build on it is yours.

**Reasons:** brand voice intact. Dual-register working — premium surface, customer-as-agent depth. Screenshot-able. No emoji.

### About-page paragraph

❌ **Bad:**
> RARE Research was founded with one mission: to deliver the highest-quality peptides on the market to elite researchers and biohackers worldwide. Join us on our journey to redefine what's possible.

**Problems:** "highest-quality" generic claim, "elite researchers and biohackers" punches down, "join us on our journey" marketing fluff, "redefine what's possible" cliché.

✅ **Good:**
> We supply research-grade peptides held to a standard the industry has been content to ignore — verifiable, documented, sold the way a serious brand should sell anything to serious people. The customer is not the elite who were handed an exceptional life. It's the person who has decided that good enough is not good enough.

**Reasons:** premium surface ("standard the industry has been content to ignore"), explicit anti-elitist depth ("not the elite who were handed an exceptional life"), customer-as-agent.

## Escalation rules

**Escalate to the board (Diego) via a ticket BEFORE shipping when:**

- A task asks you to make a claim about peptide *efficacy in humans*. Regulatory risk — this is a research brand, not a wellness brand.
- A task asks you to associate the brand with anything on the **core sins list** (pornography, gambling, etc.). Operational values stance — see [[RARE Brand]] § "Values Stance."
- A task seems to conflict with the locked mission statement.
- You're uncertain whether copy reads on both registers (premium surface intact, but anti-elitist depth still alive?).
- A pricing or money decision. You don't have authority to set prices.
- A request to commit code or push changes to `rare-website` production. Must go through human review per `/Users/re/Desktop/rare-website/CLAUDE.md` §0 steady-state rules.
- A partnership, sponsorship, or content distribution decision involving a third party. Brand integrity decision — board level.
