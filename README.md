# RARE Brand SKILL

Operational brand context for AI agents working in or for **RARE Research** and **RARE Lifestyle** — designed to load at agent runtime via [Paperclip](https://paperclip.ing/) or any compatible orchestration system.

**→ [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md)** is the file you actually load.

## What this is

A distilled operating manual for agents — the minimum context an agent needs to act on-brand without supervision:

- The locked **RARE Research mission statement** (root of every task's goal ancestry)
- What **"rare" means** — the maximization-not-elitism thesis
- The **dual-register operating principle** (premium surface, anti-elitist depth) and 5 hard rules for every output
- **Voice guidelines** — words to use, words to avoid, capitalization, required disclaimers
- **Worked examples** (good vs bad copy) across product descriptions, customer support replies, social captions, and about-page paragraphs
- **Escalation rules** — when to flag to the board before shipping
- **Brand assets quick reference** — fonts, color tokens, visual rules

## RARE Research mission

> To serve those pursuing rare — research-grade peptides held to a standard the industry has been content to ignore, for people who refuse to settle for what they were handed. Rare is not where you start. It's how you decide to live.

## Usage

Point your agent's runtime skill configuration at this repo's raw URL, or copy `SKILL.md` into the agent's SKILL file slot. In Paperclip, this skill should be loaded onto **every agent operating in any RARE company** — CEO, CMO, COO, Customer Support, Content/Copy, and any future role.

The worked examples are the load-bearing piece. Agents pattern-match on concrete good-vs-bad pairs better than they parse abstract rules.
